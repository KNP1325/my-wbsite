WHO I AM

Hello, I'm Khanyisile

My name is Khanyisile Maroping. I was born and raised in Katlehong, Johannesburg, South Africa. I am currently a student at Walter Sisulu University, where I am pursuing my Advanced Diploma in Application Development.

I have always been interested in technology and how it can be used to make everyday life easier. Through my studies, I have been building my knowledge in programming, software development and problem-solving.

I enjoy learning new things and challenging myself, especially when I get the opportunity to turn an idea into something that actually works.

One of my goals is to continue improving my technical skills and gain more experience by working on real-world projects.

I also want to become more confident, creative and independent as a developer. For me, being an independent developer means being able to take an idea, explore different ways of solving a problem, learn from my mistakes and work towards a solution on my own.

I know that I still have a lot to learn, but I am excited about the journey ahead. I want to keep growing, gaining experience and becoming a developer who can use technology to create useful and meaningful solutions.
