MY PASSION

Netball

More than a hobby, it's something I enjoy.

Netball is one of the activities I am most passionate about. I enjoy the excitement of the game, the teamwork involved and the challenge of always trying to improve.

Playing netball has taught me valuable lessons about teamwork, communication, discipline, commitment and perseverance. It has also taught me to stay focused under pressure and to keep going even when things do not go according to plan.

I enjoy how netball brings people together and how every player has an important role to play. Working together, supporting one another and trusting your teammates are important parts of the game.

Netball gives me a good balance between my academic life and my personal life. It allows me to stay active, clear my mind and enjoy something I am passionate about.
